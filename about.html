About Our Memorial

Preserving the historical legacy and remembrance of our martyrs

Our Origin and Purpose

The Baluch Martyrs Memorial was created as an independent historical archive and digital sanctuary. Across generations of hardship and historical conflict in Baluchistan, thousands of brave individuals gave their lives for the dignity, survival, and freedom of their people.

In many official historical accounts, the personal stories of these individuals are omitted or forgotten. This memorial exists so that no name is erased from memory. Each person recorded here had a family, a community, an ancestry, and a life story. We gather and preserve these records so that their memory remains accessible to future generations forever.

To remember is an act of human dignity. To preserve history is a duty to truth and future generations.

What We Document

The memorial serves as an open historical reference for families, historians, and researchers. Our documentation focuses on preserving the human truth behind each life:

Biographical Records

Recording full names, parentage, birthplace, and biographical accounts that honor each individual with dignity.

Historical Timelines

Preserving precise dates of martyrdom to create a reliable chronological register of events across modern Baluch history.

Photographic Preservation

Restoring and cataloging historical portraits, photographs, and personal documents to safeguard them from loss.

Geographic Documentation

Documenting records across all districts and historical regions of Baluchistan, preserving the local context of each loss.

Community Submissions and Verification

This archive is maintained through the collaborative efforts of families, local elders, and historical researchers. When family members or acquaintances submit a profile through our portal, each record is reviewed to maintain historical consistency, prevent duplicate entries, and ensure accurate details.

Once verified, the profile is permanently recorded in the public memorial gallery and integrated into our demographic records for educational and historical study.

Our Commitment to Remembrance and Peace

The Baluch Martyrs Memorial is exclusively a commemorative and historical archive. It is not an instrument of politics or violence. We do not promote hostility, division, or armed conflict against any people or nation.

Our work is guided by the universal human right to remember lost loved ones, document historical truth, and provide comfort to families who have endured personal loss. Every nation and culture honors those who gave their lives for their people, and the Baluch nation deserves an enduring, peaceful sanctuary to remember its fallen.
